# How to Get the Most Out of This Repository

To maximize your learning:

- Read the theory behind each topic.
- Reproduce every command in your own lab.
- Take notes while practicing.
- Complete the recommended labs.
- Avoid relying on walkthroughs unless you are completely stuck.
- Repeat difficult topics until they become second nature.
- Build your own methodology instead of memorizing commands.

Remember that penetration testing is primarily a practical discipline—consistent hands-on practice is far more valuable than simply reading documentation.

# Recommended Certification Progression

After earning the **eJPT**, consider progressing through certifications based on your interests and experience:

1. **INE eJPT** – Entry-level penetration testing.
2. **INE eCPPT** – Advanced penetration testing and reporting.
3. **TCM PNPT** – Professional penetration testing with Active Directory.
4. **Zero-Point CRTO** – Red Team operations and C2.
5. **OffSec OSCP** – Industry-standard offensive security certification.
6. **OSEP / OSWE / OSDA** – Advanced specialization paths.

# Final Thoughts

Cybersecurity is a field of continuous learning. New vulnerabilities, attack techniques, and defensive technologies emerge every day, making curiosity and consistent practice essential qualities for every penetration tester.

Use this repository as a living knowledge base. Continue updating your notes, documenting new techniques, refining your methodology, and recording lessons learned from labs, Capture The Flag challenges, and real-world engagements.

The goal is not only to pass the **INE eJPT** certification but also to build the habits and technical foundation needed for a successful career in offensive security.

Good luck, keep learning, and happy hacking!
